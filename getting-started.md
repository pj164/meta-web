<div class="start-lead">
  <p>You do not need perfectly organized records to begin. We will help you identify what is ready and what is still needed.</p>
</div>

<ol class="start-grid">
  <li>
    <strong>Your bookkeeping file</strong>
    <span>Access to QuickBooks, Zoho Books, or Xero.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Accounts in scope</strong>
    <span>The bank and credit-card accounts to include.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Prior records</strong>
    <span>Your most recently completed month—or whatever is available.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Relevant documents</strong>
    <span>Receipts, invoices, and loan or lease documents when needed.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>A point of contact</strong>
    <span>Someone who can answer the occasional transaction question.</span>
  </li>
</ol>
