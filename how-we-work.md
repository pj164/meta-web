<div class="process-lead">
  <p>A consistent monthly rhythm keeps the work moving without making bookkeeping feel complicated.</p>
  <p>QuickBooks · Zoho Books · Xero</p>
</div>

<ol class="process-steps">
  <li>
    <strong>Bring in activity</strong>
    <span>Gather the month’s transactions and see what changed.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Organize and match</strong>
    <span>Suggest categories, keep accounts consistent, and review the details.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Resolve questions</strong>
    <span>Investigate unusual items and send one concise follow-up list.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Reconcile and review</strong>
    <span>Match statements, read the reports, and prepare the summary.</span>
  </li>
</ol>

<section class="review-pair">
  <div>
    <h2>Technology handles repetition</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Reads statements and receipts</li>
      <li>Suggests categories from your history</li>
      <li>Flags possible duplicates and unusual activity</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h2>A person adds context</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Reviews categories and flagged items</li>
      <li>Checks each reconciliation</li>
      <li>Reads the reports and prepares follow-up questions</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="process-handoff">
  <div>
    <h2>What we may need</h2>
    <p>An occasional receipt or a quick explanation of an unfamiliar transaction. We keep questions concise and organized.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h2>What you receive</h2>
    <p>Reconciled books, reviewed financial statements, a short summary, and any questions still open.</p>
  </div>

</section>
